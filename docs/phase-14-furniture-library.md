# Phase 14 — reusable furniture model library

Initial authored library: 15 original procedural mesh designs, each in high and low detail, exported as separate content-addressed GLBs with metric dimensions, PBR materials, source brief page references, checksums and triangle counts. These are reference-inspired visualization concepts, not manufacturer models, surveyed dimensions or client-approved photoreal assets.

The design references are Studio Anne's Emmanuel's Design Brief: entry ottoman p.13; curved upholstery p.14; coffee table p.15; living details pp.18–19; dining p.20; pendant p.21; beds, wardrobes and study pp.22–25; kitchen cabinetry pp.26,30–32; balcony seating p.36; vanity pp.38–39.

The initial in-house pilot replaces the existing generated Stage 4 accent chair mesh parts at the same position, rotation and fitted envelope, without a duplicate chair or manual activation. Existing source IFC meshes and the original clearance gates remain untouched. Every other asset is staged in the library; room-specific placement and richer styling belong to Phases 15–19. The high and low GLBs are independently addressable for future room streaming. They do not yet automatically switch furniture LOD in the house; no GPU or RAM performance claim follows from the library alone.

Unit tests validate finite vertex buffers, dimensions and lower-detail triangle counts, PBR materials, GLB structure and content hashes; browser tests verify library publication. Actual visual review and client/architect approval remain required. Atlas packing is reserved for compatible authored non-repeating material charts, not arbitrary repeating floor/wall maps. No stock or licensed third-party model files have been downloaded.

## Full brief re-review (46 pages)

The p.14 reference shows a cream/taupe sofa with curved arms, three loose seat cushions and dark timber trim; a one-sided cream rest sofa; and an olive barrel chair with closely spaced vertical ribs and no exposed tall feet. The previous generic chair, sofa and chaise are corrected. The p.15 coffee-table reference is an overlapping pair of dark organic tops on drum bases, not a rectangular stone slab. P.20 dining requires a curved walnut shell and upholstered seat/back with slim timber legs, plus an oval dark timber top and fluted hourglass pedestals. P.25 desk requires fluted dark cylindrical supports. P.36 balcony seating requires an open slender frame, separate light cushions and woven sides. P.21 lighting requires tapered warm shades rather than generic gold bells. P.22 requires paired headboard panels and layered bedding. These features now have specific geometry and tests; photos remain the authority for human review.

Placement audit: only existing Stage 4 accent-chair replacements are enabled in this phase. The initial high model was 1.175 m high; the corrected model uses a provisional .94 m height, keeping the existing conservative collision envelope and the original candidate placement/rotation. Each replacement must stay inside the former envelope and have floor support at all four world-space footprint corners inside the provisional sitting-room polygon, or it is rejected. Export a placement record with coordinates, rotation, rendered bounds and retained collision bounds. Existing door and continuous route gates remain mandatory. Other items remain unplaced rather than guessing IFC locations. Room outlines remain provisional; the brief itself gives no surveyed furniture coordinates and p.45 requires site verification. No claim of perfect placement or visual approval.

Real-browser QA renders the 15 exported high GLBs in a separate controlled review scene and saves a labeled PNG sheet as a CI artifact. This is evidence for comparison, not automatic aesthetic approval. A feature-name assertion or successful render is not an exact photograph match.
