## Release rule

For every furniture family: reference-page comparison, actual installed mesh/asset identity, original visible stand-in removed, floor support, orientation, scale, non-overlap, clearance, and a close-up actual-room screenshot. A visually ambiguous item stays unresolved until inspected. Preserve 76 IFC layers, 26 doors and five circulation routes. No claim of exact replicas, final room placement or client approval from green CI alone.
